# Payslip Inquiry System — Employee User Guide

## How to Access Your Payslip

1. Open the Payslip Inquiry chatbot link provided by HR.
2. Type your **last 8-digit account number** in the chat box and press **Send**.
3. When asked, type your **last name** and press **Send**.
4. Your payslip will appear on screen.

---

## Step-by-Step Example

**Step 1 — Enter your account number:**
> Type: `10057788` → Send

**Step 2 — Enter your last name:**
> Type: `Santos` → Send

**Step 3 — Choose which payslip to view:**
- Click **Latest Payslip** to view the most recent one.
- Click **View Previous Payslip** to select an older date from the dropdown.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is my account number?**
A: It is the last 8 digits of your employee account number. You can find it on your previous payslip or ask HR.

**Q: My last name is not being accepted. What should I do?**
A: Make sure you are typing your last name exactly as it appears in the company records (e.g., `Dela Cruz`, not `dela cruz`). If it still does not work, contact HR/Payroll.

**Q: I cannot find my account number.**
A: Contact HR/Payroll for assistance.

**Q: How often is the payslip updated?**
A: Payslips are uploaded every cutoff period. If your latest payslip is not yet available, please wait for the HR/Payroll team to upload the data.

**Q: Is my payslip information safe?**
A: Yes. Only you can view your own payslip — the system requires both your account number and last name before showing any data.

**Q: Can I view previous payslips?**
A: Yes. After viewing your latest payslip, click **View Previous Payslip** to select an older date.
